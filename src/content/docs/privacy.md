_Last updated: 7 November 2026_

This policy explains what information craft.wiki ("the wiki") handles. ArtCraft ("we", "us") runs the wiki and is responsible for this information. Contact: [hello@storyteller.ai](mailto:hello@storyteller.ai).

## Reading the wiki

The wiki has no analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts, and sets no cookies for readers. Its fonts and search are served from the wiki itself. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising.

The site is hosted on [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). As with any website, Cloudflare processes your IP address and request details to deliver pages and protect the site from attacks. Cloudflare may keep short-term logs for these purposes.

## Signing in to edit

Only editors sign in. Readers never need to.

**Discord sign-in.** When you choose **Sign In Using Discord**, Discord asks you to share your account's basic profile and your membership of the ArtCraft server. We use this only to check that you have the Senior Contributor role. We receive your Discord user ID, username, display name, and profile picture. We do not receive your email address or password.

We do not store this information in a database. It is placed in a signed session token, kept in your browser's local storage, which expires after 8 hours. During sign-in, a cookie called `cw_auth_state` is set for up to 10 minutes to protect against forged sign-in requests. It is strictly necessary and is removed once sign-in finishes.

**GitHub sign-in.** If you sign in with a GitHub access token instead, it is kept in your browser's local storage and passed to GitHub when you save changes. We do not store it.

You can sign out, or clear the site's data in your browser, at any time.

## Edit history is public

The wiki's content lives in a public GitHub repository. When you save a change, its record includes your name: your Discord display name and username for Discord sign-in, or your GitHub account for GitHub sign-in. This history is public, and is kept to show who wrote what, as the content license requires. Ask us if you need your name removed from a past edit, and we will do what we reasonably can. Removing content from Git history is not always possible.

GitHub's handling of your data is covered by the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Discord's is covered by the [Discord Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).

## Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

The wiki does not track readers across sites, so there is nothing for Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signals to turn off. We honor them anyway: we will never use them to identify you.

## Children

The wiki is not directed at children under 13, and editors must be 13 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has signed in or contributed, email us and we will remove their information.

## Your rights in the United States

Depending on your state of residence, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. To make a request, email [hello@storyteller.ai](mailto:hello@storyteller.ai). We may need to confirm your identity first, and we will not treat you differently for making a request.

## Visitors from the EU, UK, and elsewhere

If data protection law such as the GDPR applies to you, we rely on our legitimate interests to process the information above: running a wiki with open editing, making clear who wrote each change, and keeping the site secure. You may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your information, or to object to it. Email us to make a request. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Our hosting and service providers may process information in the United States and other countries.
