## Contact

Email [hello@storyteller.ai](mailto:hello@storyteller.ai) for questions about the wiki, privacy requests, or legal notices.

For help with an ArtCraft app, see the [ArtCraft website](https://getartcraft.com) or ask in the ArtCraft Discord server.

## Report a mistake

If a guide is wrong or out of date, email us with the page address and what needs changing. You can also [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/storytold/craft-wiki/issues).

## Help improve the wiki

- **Senior Contributors** in the ArtCraft Discord server can edit guides directly. Choose **Edit wiki** at the top of any page and sign in with Discord.
- **Anyone with a GitHub account** can suggest changes. The editor saves your draft to your own copy of the repository and opens a pull request for ArtCraft to review.

By contributing, you agree to the [Terms of use](/terms/), including the license for contributions.
