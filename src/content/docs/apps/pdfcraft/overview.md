PdfCraft is an app for working with PDF files. You can read them, search them, add comments, reorder and combine pages, fill in and sign forms, and protect them with a password. It runs on Windows, macOS, Linux, FreeBSD and in a web browser. It works offline, with no account and no telemetry. It is free and open source (MIT or Apache-2.0), and made by the ArtCraft team.

:::note[Alpha]
PdfCraft is in alpha. Reading, commenting, organizing pages and filling forms work well. Editing the existing text of a PDF, turning office documents into PDFs, and exporting to Word are still weak. Keep a copy of important files, and tell us what breaks on [GitHub](https://github.com/storytold/pdfcraft/issues) or [Discord](https://discord.gg/artcraft).
:::

## What it does

- **Read**: single-page or two-page view, continuous scrolling, full screen and read mode, a dark theme, find and advanced search, bookmarks, thumbnails and layers.
- **Comment**: sticky notes, highlights, underlines, strikethroughs, text boxes, freehand drawing, shapes and stamps, all listed in the Comments panel.
- **Organize pages**: rotate, delete, insert, replace, extract, duplicate and crop pages, combine several files into one, or split one into several. See [Organize pages](/apps/pdfcraft/organize-pages/).
- **Forms and signing**: fill in forms, add your signature or initials, make your own forms, and sign with a digital ID. See [Forms and signing](/apps/pdfcraft/forms-and-signing/).
- **Protect**: password protection, redaction, and removing hidden information.
- **Convert**: export to Word, PowerPoint, RTF, HTML, text or images, make PDFs from images, and reduce file size.
- **Scan & OCR**: recognize text in scanned pages, so you can search and copy it.

## Your files

PdfCraft opens and saves **PDF** files. When you save over a file, PdfCraft adds your changes to the end of it, so the original content is kept. It can also make a PDF from images, from other files, or from what's on the clipboard.

## What you need

- Windows (64-bit, ARM or 32-bit), macOS (Apple silicon or Intel), Linux, or FreeBSD.
- Or a modern web browser, for the web version.
