## 1. Install PdfCraft

The quickest way is [ArtCraft Launcher](https://github.com/storytold/craft-launcher/releases/latest): install it, then press **Install** on PdfCraft's card. To download PdfCraft on its own, see [Install and update](/apps/pdfcraft/install-update/).

## 2. Open a PDF

PdfCraft starts on its **Home** page, with recommended tools and your recent files. Click **Open file**, press **Ctrl+O** (**⌘O** on a Mac), or drop a PDF anywhere in the window. Each PDF opens in its own tab at the top. The house button at the top left brings Home back.

## 3. Find your way around

- The menus at the top left are **File**, **Edit**, **View**, **Sign**, **Window** and **Help**. On a Mac they are in the menu bar at the top of the screen.
- **All tools**, **Edit**, **Convert** and **E-Sign**, next to them, change which tools the panel on the left shows.
- The buttons down the right edge open side panels: comments, bookmarks, page thumbnails, form fields, layers and attachments.
- The controls at the bottom right show the page number and change the zoom.
- **Find tools and commands** at the top (**Ctrl+K**, **⌘K** on a Mac) finds any tool or command by name.

![The Find tools and commands box with "page" typed in it, listing matching commands such as Rotate pages clockwise, Delete pages, Insert pages from file and Extract pages, each with the tool it belongs to](/assets/uploads/pdfcraft/palette.webp)

## 4. Move through the document

Scroll, or type a page number in the box at the bottom right. **Ctrl+F** (**⌘F**) finds text. **Ctrl+1** shows the page at its actual size, **Ctrl+2** fits its width, and **Ctrl+0** fits the whole page. **View › Page display** switches between single page and two-page view.

## 5. Add a comment

Choose **Add comments** in the tools panel. Select some text and highlight it, or add a sticky note where you click. Every comment appears in the **Comments** panel on the right, where you can reply to it.

## 6. Save

Press **Ctrl+S** (**⌘S** on a Mac) to save the PDF, or **Ctrl+Shift+S** to save a copy under another name.

PdfCraft saves a copy of unsaved changes every minute. If it closes unexpectedly, it offers to recover them the next time it starts.
