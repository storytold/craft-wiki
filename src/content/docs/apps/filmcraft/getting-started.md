## 1. Install FilmCraft

The quickest way is [ArtCraft Launcher](https://github.com/storytold/craft-launcher/releases/latest): install it, then press **Install** on FilmCraft's card. To download FilmCraft on its own, see [Install and update](/apps/filmcraft/install-update/).

## 2. Open FilmCraft

FilmCraft opens on a demo project, so you can look around before importing anything. Along the top are the **Home** button and three tabs:

- **Import** brings media into your project.
- **Edit** is where you cut, grade and mix.
- **Export** renders your sequence to a file.

The menus (**File**, **Edit**, **Clip**, **Sequence**, **Markers**, **Graphics and Titles**, **View**, **Window** and **Help**) sit next to the tabs. On a Mac they are in the menu bar at the top of the screen.

![The Assembly workspace: the Project panel with clip thumbnails at the top left, Source and Program monitors at the top right, and a timeline with video on V1 and V2, dialogue on A1 and music on A2](/assets/uploads/filmcraft/assembly.webp)

## 3. Start a project and import your media

1. Choose **File › New › Project…** to start your own project.
2. Choose **File › Import…** (**Ctrl+I**, or **⌘I** on a Mac) and pick your video, audio and still files. You can also drop files or folders onto the window.
3. The files appear in the **Project** panel.

FilmCraft doesn't copy your media into the project. Keep the files where they are, or FilmCraft won't find them next time.

## 4. Make a sequence

In the **Project** panel, right-click a clip and choose **New Sequence From Clip**. FilmCraft makes a sequence with the same settings as the clip, and puts its picture on a video track such as **V1** and its sound on an audio track such as **A1**.

To add more clips, drag them from the **Project** panel onto the timeline.

## 5. Cut

- Press **Space** to play and stop. **J**, **K** and **L** play backwards, stop and play forward.
- Press **C** for the **Razor Tool** and click a clip to cut it in two. Press **V** to go back to the **Selection Tool**.
- Select a clip and press **Backspace** to remove it and leave a gap (**Edit › Clear**), or **Shift+Delete** to remove it and close the gap (**Edit › Ripple Delete**).
- **Edit › Undo** (**Ctrl+Z**, or **⌘Z** on a Mac) undoes a mistake.

For fades and transitions, see [Workflows](/apps/filmcraft/workflows/#effects-transitions-and-keyframes).

## 6. Save

Choose **File › Save** (**Ctrl+S**, or **⌘S** on a Mac). FilmCraft also saves an auto-save copy every few minutes.

## 7. Export

1. Click the **Export** tab.
2. Enter a **File Name** and a **Location**.
3. Leave **Format** at **H.264** for an MP4 that plays almost anywhere.
4. Click **Export**.

The export runs in the background, so you can keep editing. For other formats, see [Workflows](/apps/filmcraft/workflows/#export).
