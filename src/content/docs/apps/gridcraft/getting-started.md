## 1. Install GridCraft

The quickest way is [ArtCraft Launcher](https://github.com/storytold/craft-launcher/releases/latest): install it, then press **Install** on GridCraft's card. To download GridCraft on its own, see [Install and update](/apps/gridcraft/install-update/).

## 2. Open GridCraft

GridCraft opens on a blank workbook called **Book1**. To open a file instead, press **Ctrl+O** (**⌘O** on a Mac), or drag the file onto the window.

To look around before typing anything, open a sample. Click **File**, then choose **Household Budget**, **Quarterly Sales Dashboard** or **Gradebook**. Each one uses formulas, formatting and charts you can take apart.

![The Household Budget sample on the Home tab: a budget table with accounting number formats, red highlights for overspent rows, a color scale on the % Used column, and a bar chart comparing budget with actual](/assets/uploads/gridcraft/budget.webp)

## 3. Enter data and formulas

Click a cell and type. Press **Enter** to move down, or **Tab** to move right.

To add a formula, start with `=`, for example `=SUM(B2:B10)`. While you type a formula, click or drag across cells to put their references into it. Each reference gets its own color in the cell and on the sheet.

A few things that work as you would expect:

- Drag the small square at the corner of the selection (the fill handle) to fill a series or copy a formula.
- **Ctrl+D** fills down, **Ctrl+R** fills right.
- **F2** edits the active cell.
- **Ctrl+Z** undoes, **Ctrl+Y** redoes.

## 4. Turn your data into a table

Click inside your data and press **Ctrl+T**, or choose **Format as Table** on the **Home** tab. A table gets banded rows, filter buttons on its header row, and grows when you add rows under it.

## 5. Save

Press **Ctrl+S** (**⌘S** on a Mac). The first time, GridCraft asks where to save. It saves as .xlsx, so others can open your workbook in most spreadsheet apps.

To save after every change without thinking about it, turn on **AutoSave** at the top left of the window. It works for workbooks that already have a file.

## 6. Share as a PDF

Choose **File › Export › Create PDF**. The PDF keeps the sheet's page layout. To set margins, orientation and what fits on a page first, see [Print and export](/apps/gridcraft/workflows/#print-and-export).
