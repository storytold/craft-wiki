DeckCraft's ribbon groups its tools into tabs: **Home**, **Insert**, **Draw**, **Design**, **Transitions**, **Animations**, **Slide Show**, **Record**, **Review** and **View**. More tabs appear when you select something, such as **Shape Format**, **Picture Format** or **Table Design**. If you can't find a command, press **Ctrl+Shift+P** (**⇧⌘P** on a Mac) and type its name.

## Organize slides

- **Home › New Slide** adds a slide; its arrow picks a layout. **Layout** changes the current slide's layout, and **Reset** puts its placeholders back.
- **Home › Section** groups slides into named sections. Click a section's name in the thumbnails to fold it away.
- Drag thumbnails to reorder slides. **View › Slide Sorter** shows them all at once, with each slide's transition.
- **Slide Show › Hide Slide** keeps a slide in the file but skips it when you present.

![Slide Sorter view: eight slide thumbnails in two rows, each with its number and the name of its transition, such as fade, push, wipe and morph, beneath it](/assets/uploads/deckcraft/slide-sorter.webp)

## Change the design

- On the **Design** tab, pick a theme. **Colors**, **Fonts** and **Effects** change one part of it. **Customize Colors…** and **Customize Fonts…** make your own.
- **Format Background** sets a solid, gradient, picture or pattern background.
- **Slide Size** switches between **Widescreen (16:9)** and **Standard (4:3)**, or sets a custom size.
- **Design Ideas** shows the current slide in each theme and in other layouts. Click one to use it. It works offline.

To change the layouts themselves, choose **View › Slide Master**. Changes there apply to every slide that uses that layout. **Notes Master** and **Handout Master** set up printed notes and handouts.

## Add shapes, pictures and more

On the **Insert** tab:

- **Shapes**: preset shapes with yellow handles to adjust them, lines and connectors that stay attached when you move shapes. Select two or more shapes to combine them with **Shape Format › Merge Shapes**.
- **Pictures**: add pictures (PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, BMP or TIFF), then crop them and adjust them on the **Picture Format** tab.
- **Table** and **Chart**: on the **Chart Design** tab, **Edit Data** changes a chart's numbers and **Change Chart Type** its kind.
- **SmartArt**, **Icons**, **WordArt**, **Equation** and **Symbol**.
- **Video** and **Audio**: they play in the slide show. Select one for the **Playback** tab.

Select a shape and click **Format Pane** on the **Shape Format** tab for its fill, line, shadow, glow, soft edges and reflection. **Selection Pane** lists everything on the slide, so you can pick, hide or rename it.

![The Shape Format tab and the Format Shape pane: a selected shape has a blue-to-red linear gradient, and the pane shows its preset gradients, direction, angle and gradient stops](/assets/uploads/deckcraft/format-pane.webp)

**Home › Format** (Format Painter) copies formatting from one shape or piece of text to another.

## Draw with a pen

The **Draw** tab has pens, a pencil, a highlighter and an eraser for writing on slides.

## Add transitions

On the **Transitions** tab, pick a transition for the current slide. **Effect Options** changes its direction, **Duration** its speed, and **Apply To All** uses it on every slide. Set **After** to move on by itself after a time, and add a sound. **Morph** moves shapes smoothly between two slides that share them.

## Add animations

Select a shape or text box, then pick an effect on the **Animations** tab: entrance, emphasis, exit or a motion path.

- **Effect Options** sets the direction, or animates text one paragraph at a time.
- Choose when it starts (**On Click**, **With Previous** or **After Previous**), and its **Duration** and **Delay**.
- **Animation Pane** lists every animation on the slide in order. **Animation Painter** copies an animation to another shape.
- **Preview** plays the slide's animations.

## Review

On the **Review** tab:

- **Spelling** (**F7**) checks the whole presentation. Misspelled words are also underlined as you type.
- **Thesaurus** (**Shift+F7**) suggests other words.
- **New Comment** (**Ctrl+Alt+M**) adds a comment. **Show Comments** opens the comments pane.
- **Compare** shows the differences from a revised copy of the presentation, slide by slide, so you can accept or reject each revised slide.
- **Check Accessibility** finds things that make slides hard to follow with assistive technology, such as pictures without alt text.

## Change the view

On the **View** tab: **Normal**, **Outline View**, **Slide Sorter**, **Notes Page** and **Reading View**. You can also show the **Ruler**, **Gridlines** (**Shift+F9**) and **Guides** (**Alt+F9**), and turn on **Dark Mode**. The buttons at the bottom right of the window switch views and zoom.

![DeckCraft in dark mode: the ribbon and slide thumbnails are dark, and the slide shows a grouped bar chart of clear nights per month for three places](/assets/uploads/deckcraft/dark-mode.webp)

## Export and print

- **File › Export › Create PDF Document** makes a PDF of the slides. **Create Handouts** puts several slides on each page.
- **File › Export › Change File Type** saves as .pptx, .deckcraft, an outline (.txt or .rtf), or the current slide as a PNG or JPEG picture.
- The **Export** button in the Quick Access Toolbar at the top left also makes PNG pictures of every slide, notes pages as a PDF, or an **Animated GIF** of the whole deck.
- **File › Print** (**Ctrl+P**) prints slides, notes pages or handouts.

Converting between formats can lose some formatting. To share a presentation that must look the same everywhere, export a PDF.

## Preferences

Choose **File › Options** (**Ctrl+,**) to change the measurement units, interface size, dark appearance, smart guides, snapping, AutoCorrect, and your name for comments.

## Keyboard shortcuts

On a Mac, use **⌘** in place of **Ctrl** and **⌥** in place of **Alt**.

| Shortcut | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Ctrl+N** | New presentation |
| **Ctrl+Shift+N** | New slide |
| **Ctrl+O**, **Ctrl+S** | Open, save |
| **Ctrl+Shift+S** | Save as |
| **Ctrl+Z**, **Ctrl+Y** | Undo, redo |
| **Ctrl+D** | Duplicate |
| **Ctrl+B**, **Ctrl+I**, **Ctrl+U** | Bold, italic, underline |
| **Ctrl+L**, **Ctrl+E**, **Ctrl+R**, **Ctrl+J** | Align left, center, right, justify |
| **Ctrl+Shift+.**, **Ctrl+Shift+,** | Bigger, smaller text |
| **Ctrl+K** | Insert a link |
| **Ctrl+Alt+G**, **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+G** | Group, ungroup |
| **Ctrl+F**, **Ctrl+H** | Find, replace |
| **F5**, **Shift+F5** | Present from the start, from the current slide |
| **F7** | Spelling |
| **Ctrl+Shift+P** | Search commands |
| **Ctrl+F1** | Collapse the ribbon |
