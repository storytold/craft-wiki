## 1. Install DeckCraft

The quickest way is [ArtCraft Launcher](https://github.com/storytold/craft-launcher/releases/latest): install it, then press **Install** on DeckCraft's card. To download DeckCraft on its own, see [Install and update](/apps/deckcraft/install-update/).

## 2. Open DeckCraft

DeckCraft opens on the **File** tab's **Home** page. From here you can start a **Blank Presentation**, pick a theme or a template, or open a recent presentation. **File** at the left end of the ribbon brings this page back at any time.

To look around before you make anything, open **Sample deck: Northern Lights**. It uses sections, shapes, a chart, a table, transitions and animations.

## 3. Add slides

- On the **Home** tab, click **New Slide** (**Ctrl+Shift+N**, **⇧⌘N** on a Mac). The arrow under it picks a layout, such as Title and Content or Two Content.
- Click in a placeholder and type. **Tab** indents a bullet, **Shift+Tab** brings it back.
- To change a slide's layout later, use **Layout** on the **Home** tab.
- Type speaker notes in **Click to add notes** under the slide.

## 4. Pick a theme

On the **Design** tab, pick a theme to change the colors and fonts of every slide at once. **Colors** and **Fonts** change just one of them. **Slide Size** switches between **Widescreen (16:9)** and **Standard (4:3)**.

## 5. Present

Press **F5** to present from the first slide, or **Shift+F5** from the current one. **Present** at the top right of the window also starts from the current slide. Press **Esc** to stop. See [Slide shows](/apps/deckcraft/slide-shows/) for Presenter View and the keys you can use while presenting.

## 6. Save

Press **Ctrl+S** (**⌘S** on a Mac). A new presentation is saved as a **.deckcraft** file, DeckCraft's own format, which keeps everything. To share it with people who use other presentation apps, choose **File › Save As** and pick **PowerPoint Presentation (*.pptx)**. A presentation you opened as .pptx is saved back as .pptx.

DeckCraft keeps a copy of unsaved work in the background. If it closes unexpectedly, the next start opens the copy again for you to save.

## 7. Share as a PDF

Choose **File › Export › Create PDF Document**. **Create Handouts** puts several slides on each page instead.
