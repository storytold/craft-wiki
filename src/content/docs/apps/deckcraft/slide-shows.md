## Start a slide show

- **F5**, or **Slide Show › Play from Start**, starts from the first slide.
- **Shift+F5**, **Slide Show › Play from Current Slide**, or **Present** at the top right of the window starts from the slide you're on.
- **Esc** ends the show.

Double-clicking a .ppsx file starts its slide show straight away.

## Use Presenter View

**Presenter View** on the **Slide Show** tab is on by default, and needs a second display: Presenter View opens in a window of its own, so you can put it on your screen while the audience sees the slides on the other display. With one display, the show has only the slides. It shows:

- the current slide and the next one, with **Next** to move on,
- your speaker notes,
- a timer, with **Pause** and **Reset**,
- **Black** and **White** to blank the audience's screen, **Zoom** to zoom into part of a slide, **All slides** to jump to any slide, and **End Show**.

## Keys while presenting

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **→**, **↓**, **Space**, **Page Down**, **N** or **Enter** | Next animation or slide |
| **←**, **↑**, **Backspace**, **Page Up** or **P** | Previous animation or slide |
| **Home**, **End** | First slide, last slide |
| A slide number, then **Enter** | Go to that slide |
| **G** | Show all slides to pick one |
| **Z** | Zoom in |
| **B** or **.** | Black screen, and back |
| **W** or **,** | White screen, and back |
| **Ctrl+P** | Pen on and off |
| **Ctrl+L** | Laser pointer on and off |
| **Ctrl+A** | Back to the arrow pointer |
| **E** | Erase what you've drawn |
| **T**, **R** | Pause the timer, reset it (Presenter View) |
| **[**, **]** | Smaller, bigger notes (Presenter View) |
| **Esc** | End the show |

On a Mac, use **⌘** in place of **Ctrl**.

## Rehearse your timings

Choose **Slide Show › Rehearse Timings** and present as you would for real. DeckCraft records how long you spend on each slide. Then tick **Use Timings** to have the show move on by itself.

## Set up the show

**Slide Show › Set Up Slide Show** chooses the show type (presented by a speaker, browsed in a window, or at a kiosk), which slides to show, whether to loop until **Esc**, and whether to use timings. **Custom Show** makes a named show from some of the slides, in your own order. **Hide Slide** leaves a slide out of the show without deleting it.

**Show Media Controls** shows play and pause controls on videos and sounds during the show.

## Share without DeckCraft

To give people a copy they can open anywhere, export a PDF with **File › Export › Create PDF Document**, or save as a .pptx. The **Export** button at the top left also makes an animated GIF of the deck.
