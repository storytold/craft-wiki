PhotoCraft is an image editor for photos and artwork. It has layers, masks, adjustment layers, layer styles, type, vector shapes, brushes and filters, and it opens and saves layered PSD files. The menus, panels, tools and shortcuts are laid out the way most professional image editors lay them out, so if you have used one, you will find your way around. It runs on Windows, macOS, Linux, FreeBSD and in a web browser. It is free and open source (MIT or Apache-2.0), and made by the ArtCraft team.

:::note[Early alpha]
PhotoCraft is in early alpha. Much is in place, but it isn't ready to be your only image editor for daily professional work yet. Expect rough edges, keep a copy of important files, and tell us what breaks on [GitHub](https://github.com/storytold/photocraft/issues) or [Discord](https://discord.gg/artcraft).
:::

## What it does

- **Layers**: groups, clipping masks, layer and vector masks, fill layers, smart objects, blend modes, opacity and locks.
- **Adjustment layers**: Levels, Curves, Hue/Saturation, Vibrance, Black & White, Gradient Map and more. Your original pixels stay as they were.
- **Selections**: marquees, lassos, the Magic Wand, Quick Selection, Object Selection and **Select › Subject**. Smart selection runs on your computer.
- **Layer styles**: drop shadows, glows, bevels, strokes and overlays, on any layer, including text.
- **Type**: point and paragraph text that stays editable.
- **Shapes and paths**: rectangles, ellipses, polygons, lines and the Pen tools.
- **Painting and retouching**: brushes with pen pressure and tilt, healing and clone tools, dodge and burn.
- **Filters**: blurs, sharpening, distortions, noise and more, with a live preview on the canvas.
- **Color**: RGB, Grayscale, CMYK and Lab documents at 8, 16 or 32 bits per channel, with color profiles.
- **Automation**: record actions, run batches, or drive PhotoCraft from the command line or an [AI agent](/apps/photocraft/automation/).

## Your files

PhotoCraft opens and saves layered **.psd** and **.psb** files, and has its own **.pcraft** format. It also opens files from other editors, such as GIMP, Krita, Paint.NET and Affinity, and works with everyday formats like PNG, JPEG, WebP and TIFF. See [File formats](/apps/photocraft/file-formats/) for the full list.

PhotoCraft works offline. It doesn't need an account.

## What you need

- Windows (64-bit, ARM or 32-bit), macOS (Apple silicon or Intel), Linux (x86_64, ARM64 or RISC-V) or FreeBSD.
- Or a modern web browser, for the web version.
