ArtCraft Studio is a desktop app for making images, video, audio and 3D with AI models. You can start from a prompt, or build the scene first: lay out images on a 2D canvas, or pose characters, props and a camera on a 3D stage, then generate from that. It runs on Windows, macOS and Linux. It is open source (MIT or Apache-2.0), and made by the ArtCraft team.

:::note
ArtCraft Studio needs an internet connection and an ArtCraft account. The AI models run online, not on your computer. Generating with ArtCraft's models uses credits.
:::

## What it does

- **Create Image**: text to image, and prompted image editing, with a choice of image models.
- **Create Video**: animate an image or a prompt with a choice of video models.
- **Create Audio**: music and sound.
- **3D**: turn an image into a 3D object or a 3D world.
- **Image Editor**: a 2D canvas with shapes, images, brush, mask and eraser tools, to compose a picture before you generate from it.
- **3D Stage**: place characters, objects, sets and a skybox in 3D, pose them, set the camera, and capture a still or a video.
- **Edit tools**: inpainting, background removal and a video frame extractor.
- **My Library**: everything you generate or upload, in one place.

## Models and accounts

ArtCraft offers image, video, music and sound, 3D mesh and world models through your ArtCraft account, such as Nano Banana, GPT Image, FLUX, Seedream, Seedance, Kling, Veo, Suno and Hunyuan 3D. The list changes often; the model picker in the app shows what is available.

You can also connect your own **Grok** and **Midjourney** accounts. See [Workflows](/apps/artcraft-studio/workflows/#connect-grok-or-midjourney).

## Your files

Everything you make is kept in **My Library**. When you download a result, it goes to your **Downloads** folder by default. ArtCraft Studio keeps its own data in an `Artcraft` folder in your home folder.

## What you need

- Windows (64-bit), macOS (Apple silicon or Intel), or Linux (64-bit Intel or AMD, ARM64 or RISC-V).
- An internet connection and an ArtCraft account.
