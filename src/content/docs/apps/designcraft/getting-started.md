Shortcuts on this page use **Ctrl**. On a Mac, use **⌘** in place of **Ctrl**, and **Option** in place of **Alt**.

## 1. Install DesignCraft

The quickest way is [ArtCraft Launcher](https://github.com/storytold/craft-launcher/releases/latest): install it, then press **Install** on DesignCraft's card. To download DesignCraft on its own, see [Install and update](/apps/designcraft/install-update/).

## 2. Open the sample magazine

DesignCraft opens on its start screen, with three buttons: **New file**, **Open** and **Open sample magazine**. Below them are your recent files and a tile for each print page size.

Click **Open sample magazine** to see a finished layout. It has a cover, styled feature pages, one story threaded through three columns and around a pull quote, and a page of swatches. You can open it again later from **Help › Sample Document**.

![The sample magazine's cover: The Quiet Art of Layout in large white serif type over layered purple hills and a pale sun](/assets/uploads/designcraft/sample-cover.webp)

## 3. Start a document

Click **New file**, or choose **File › New Document…** (**Ctrl+N**).

1. Pick a size on the **Print**, **Web** or **Mobile** tab, or type your own **Width** and **Height**.
2. Set the number of **Pages**. Select **Facing Pages** for left and right pages side by side, as in a magazine.
3. Set **Columns**, **Column Gutter** and the **Margins**. Add **Bleed** if the document is going to a printer.
4. Select **Primary Text Frame** if you want a text frame that fills the margins on every page.
5. Click **Create**.

To skip the dialog, click one of the size tiles under **Start a new document** on the start screen.

## 4. Add text and pictures

- Choose the **Type Tool** (**T**) and drag on the page to draw a text frame, then type.
- To bring in a picture or a text file, choose **File › Place…** (**Ctrl+D**) and pick it. If an empty or picture frame is selected, the picture fills it. If not, click to place the picture at its own size, or drag to draw its frame.
- Choose the **Selection Tool** (**V**) to move and resize frames.

## 5. Save

Press **Ctrl+S**. DesignCraft saves a **.designcraft** file.

DesignCraft also saves recovery data in the background. If it closes unexpectedly, it reopens your unsaved documents the next time it starts.

## 6. Export a PDF

Choose **File › Export PDF…**, pick a **Preset**, click **OK** and choose where to save. **Desktop Printing** suits a home printer, **Screen and Email** a smaller file to send, and **Commercial Printing** or **PDF/X-4** a print shop. See [Export a PDF](/apps/designcraft/workflows/#export-a-pdf).
